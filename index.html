About Me

Hi, this is Kunyi (Mark) Ma 马坤一, a Mathematician in PDE Analysis.
I'm a Columbia University Mathematics PhD Candidate. My advisors are Daniela De Silva and Ovidiu Savin.
I graduated from New York University Shanghai in January 2024 with a Bachelor of Science in Honors Mathematics and Data Science (Mathematics Concentration). My Senior Thesis mentors are Vahagn Nersesyan and Zhuang Su.
