Bouldering

I am an amateur (shamelessly) V2–V3 climber, mostly in New York and Shanghai.

New York: VITAL (West Harlem, Lower East Side).

Shanghai: Banana Climbing(金虹桥，延安西路，Banana+嘉里合集)； CAMP4 岩肆（八号桥）; Benchmark 3.0（荟聚）; GOAT 2.0 (徐家汇TPY中心).

Others include Portside Boulders (Osborne Park, Perth); Climbing District (Buttes-Chaumont, Paris); LOOK ROCK Bouldering Park (Okinawa); Banana Climbing(亲橙万象汇) and MTC 顶峰攀岩 (Wuhan); 心跳攀岩 (Nanjing).
