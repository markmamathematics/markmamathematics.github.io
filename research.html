Research

My research interests are in partial differential equations and free boundary problems.

Preprints

  1. Kunyi (Mark) Ma. The Alt-Phillips Multiple Membrane Free Boundary Problem.
    arXiv:2611.11736 [math.AP]. Preprint, 2026.
    Abstract

    Motivated by diffusion–reaction processes in porous catalysts, we study an Alt–Phillips model for ordered membranes with weighted gap interactions. The model connects adhesive harmonic junctions [DSS24] and forced multiple membranes [SY19] through the exponent 0<γ<1. For arbitrary positive weights, we prove optimal C1,β−1 regularity, where β=2/(2−γ), and obtain nontrivial β-homogeneous blowup limits through Weiss almost monotonicity. Under strict discrete concavity of the weights, we establish a gluing principle, minimality of flat single-gap cones, and C1,α free boundary regularity at points admitting such blowups. We also classify all one-dimensional vertically critical cones. For three membranes, we identify cone families that are non-minimizing for small γ and minimizing near γ=1.

  2. Kunyi (Mark) Ma. A Short Proof of Optimal Regularity for minimizers of the Alt-Phillips Problem.
    arXiv:2607.08685 [math.AP]. Preprint, 2026.
    Abstract

    We give a self-contained short proof of optimal regularity for minimizers of the Alt-Phillips Free Boundary Problem for γ∈(0,1). We adopt a dichotomy argument that originates from [DS20].

  3. Nicholas Gismondi, Kunyi (Mark) Ma, Mandon Pathak, Alexandru F. Radu. Non-unique solutions to the periodic gKdV equation.
    arXiv:2606.06916 [math.AP]. Preprint, 2026.
    Abstract

    In this paper we utilize a convex integration scheme to construct non-trivial weak solutions to the k-generalized KdV equation which lie in ⋂ε>0Ct0Lxk−ε([0,1]×𝕋) and, when k≥3, it may also be chosen in ⋂ε>0Ct0Hx12−1k−ε([0,1]×𝕋) attaining identically 0 initial data. Since our solutions do not lie in Ct0Lxk, this requires introducing a new notion of weak solution, which is in fact stronger than the classical notion of a weak solution when the nonlinearity is integrable. This result shows that a necessary condition for unconditional uniqueness for k-gKdV is that the nonlinearity lies in Ct0Lx1. In the case of KdV this is in fact also sufficient.
